// 07 — FAQ

Straight answers.

CLAIMS & FILES

What does a supplement cost?

Per-file work is priced as a flat fee or a percentage of the recovered increase — your choice at engagement. Monthly retainers are available for shops with steady volume. Your first file review is free, and if we don't see recoverable money on it, we'll tell you that too.

How fast is turnaround?

Days, not weeks. A straightforward supplement moves fastest; a multi-round audit rebuttal takes longer. Either way you get a timeline before we start, and you'll never wonder where your file is.

Do you work outside Tennessee?

Yes. Cat 4 is based in Middle Tennessee and works nationwide. Claims work is file-based and call capture is phone-based — nothing about it requires us to be in your zip code.

What do you need from me?

The estimate (PDF or ESX), photos, drying logs or moisture maps if you have them, and every piece of carrier correspondence — especially the denial letter or audit report. Imperfect documentation isn't a dealbreaker; it's most files. We work with what you have.

Do you talk to the adjuster directly?

Your call. Most contractors submit under their own letterhead with us behind the scenes — it keeps your carrier relationships yours. When a file needs a technical voice on the call, we can be there.

I'm a roofer, not a mitigation company. Can you help?

Yes. Storm and roofing supplements, O&P arguments, and scope fights follow the same playbook: documentation, standards, math. If it's an insurance file, we can work it.

OFFICE & CALLS

Is after-hours answering a call center, or someone who knows my business?

Someone working from your playbook. Before we take a single call, we build your intake script with you: your services, your service area, your pricing rules, your on-call rotation. Callers hear your company name — not a switchboard reading a generic script.

What happens when an emergency call comes in at night?

We answer live, qualify the loss — what's wet, for how long, is the water shut off — capture the address and callback number, and dispatch per your rules, straight to your on-call tech with everything they need. You get a booked emergency job with notes, not a 6am voicemail from a customer who already called someone else.

Can you integrate with my scheduling software?

In most cases, yes. If you run ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or even a shared calendar, we book directly into it. If your system is a whiteboard and a group text, that works too — jobs get delivered however you actually run your day.

What does call coverage cost?

A monthly subscription sized by call volume and hours of coverage, so a two-truck carpet shop isn't priced like a ten-crew restoration company. Tell us your trade and call pattern and we'll quote it straight — after we run the missed-call math with you, free, so you know what not answering already costs.

// 07 — FAQ

Straight answers.

What does a supplement cost?

Per-file work is priced as a flat fee or a percentage of the recovered increase — your choice at engagement. Monthly retainers are available for shops with steady volume. Your first file review is free, and if we don't see recoverable money on it, we'll tell you that too.

How fast is turnaround?

Days, not weeks. A straightforward supplement moves fastest; a multi-round audit rebuttal takes longer. Either way you get a timeline before we start, and you'll never wonder where your file is.

Do you work outside Tennessee?

Yes. Cat 4 is based in Middle Tennessee and works files nationwide. Everything is remote: you send the documentation, we send back the work.

What do you need from me?

The estimate (PDF or ESX), photos, drying logs or moisture maps if you have them, and every piece of carrier correspondence — especially the denial letter or audit report. Imperfect documentation isn't a dealbreaker; it's most files. We work with what you have.

Do you talk to the adjuster directly?

Your call. Most contractors submit under their own letterhead with us behind the scenes — it keeps your carrier relationships yours. When a file needs a technical voice on the call, we can be there.

I'm a roofer, not a mitigation company. Can you help?

Yes. Storm and roofing supplements, O&P arguments, and scope fights follow the same playbook: documentation, standards, math. If it's an insurance file, we can work it.
